How to determine similarity

A hybrid taste vector

No single signal is trustworthy enough. Exact overlap rewards shared favorites but misses spiritually similar picks. Embeddings catch meaning but can flatten eccentricity. The strongest model blends both, downweights giant box sets, and explains every match in plain language.

01Build one profile per film from synopsis, director, country, language, movement, decade, and collection notes.
02Embed every film, then create a rare-pick-weighted centroid for each Closet visit.
03Deduplicate films within a visit and cap box-set influence so one purchase cannot dominate.
04Blend cosine similarity with exact overlap, director affinity, and distribution distance.
05Project the pairwise matrix into two dimensions with UMAP for the overview map.
06Generate evidence: shared films, adjacent films, common directors, and strongest differences.

How to show it

Twelve directions

Pick a tab to compare overview maps, pairwise explainers, analytic tools, and more editorial interpretations. The strongest product could combine two or three instead of choosing only one.

01 / Recommended overview

Semantic constellation

Every picker is a star. UMAP places semantically similar taste profiles nearby; subtle links reveal only the strongest relationships.

Best for
Immediate exploration of all 396 visits.
Interaction
Search, zoom, hover, click to pin, then open evidence in a side drawer.
Strength
Feels magical while preserving a sense of the whole archive.
Risk
Two-dimensional distance can look more authoritative than it really is.

A second way to compare

Four product packages

A / Fastest MVP

Matches + evidence

Ship explainable match cards first. It answers the core question cleanly and gives us a way to validate the scoring model before drawing a map.

B / Recommended

Constellation + orbit

Use the constellation as the main overview. Clicking a picker opens their neighbor orbit and a factual evidence drawer.

C / Editorial

Atlas + metro

Name the major communities and turn them into guided cinematic journeys. Strongest identity, most ongoing editorial work.

D / Research mode

Matrix + fingerprints

Offer a precise heatmap and pairwise feature comparison for people who want to interrogate the model instead of simply browsing.

My vote Start with explainable match cards, then graduate to the constellation once the scores feel right.
